Mixmap

See your set as a space.

Drop an Ableton Live project and every track becomes a shape of light in a 3D room. You can see where each sound lives, what collides, and which parts of the spectrum are empty, bar by bar.

Height
Frequency, from sub at the floor to air at the top
Width
Stereo width and pan
Depth
Level. Louder sounds come forward
Color
The notes being played, on the circle of fifths

For the most accurate map

  1. Save your set, then run File → Collect All and Save so the samples sit in the project folder.
  2. Export stems: File → Export Audio/Video, set Rendered Track to All Individual Tracks, start at bar 1, and save them into the project folder.
  3. Drop the whole project folder here.

Without stems, audio tracks are measured from their samples and MIDI tracks are estimated from their notes.

Drop your project folder

The folder with your .als file. Stems and samples inside it are picked up automatically. You can also drop a saved .mixmap.json file.

Options

Private by design. Everything runs in your browser. Your audio never leaves your computer.
